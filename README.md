## Project Overview

This project analyses the sales performance of a single product across promotional and non-promotional periods.

The analysis focuses on pricing, sales volume, profitability, and customer response to price changes. Price Elasticity of Demand (PED) is used to evaluate how changes in selling price affected quantity demanded during selected promotional periods.

The overall objective is to determine whether the product performs better when sold at a promotional price or at its regular price.

## Business Questions

The analysis was designed to answer the following questions:

1. What is the daily sales price per unit?
2. What is the average unit sales price of the product?
3. What is the daily percentage gross profit?
4. What is the daily percentage gross profit per unit?
5. Which periods indicate that the product was being sold on promotion?
6. What was the Price Elasticity of Demand during three selected promotional periods?
7. Does the product perform better or worse when sold at a promotional price?

## Dataset

The dataset contains historical sales information for one product, including:

* Date
* Sales Revenue
* Cost of Sales
* Quantity Sold

These variables were used to derive additional metrics required for evaluating pricing, profitability and promotional performance.

## Analysis Process

### 1. Data Inspection and Cleaning

The dataset was inspected before analysis to understand its structure and identify potential data quality issues.

The process included:

* Checking data types
* Checking for null values
* Checking for duplicate records
* Reviewing sales, cost and quantity values
* Extracting relevant date information

### 2. Exploratory Data Analysis

SQL was used to explore sales patterns and calculate the key metrics required for the case study.

The analysis included:

* Daily sales revenue
* Daily quantity sold
* Sales price per unit
* Average unit sales price
* Gross profit
* Daily gross profit percentage
* Gross profit per unit
* Daily gross profit percentage per unit
* Identification of promotional periods

### 3. Promotional Analysis

Promotional periods were identified by comparing daily unit prices with the typical selling price of the product. Three promotional periods were selected for further analysis.

Price Elasticity of Demand was then used to measure how responsive quantity demanded was to changes in price.

**Price Elasticity of Demand:**

`PED = % Change in Quantity Demanded / % Change in Price`

The promotional periods were compared with non-promotional periods to assess whether reducing the product price resulted in sufficient increases in demand and overall sales performance.
